# Visual Review Guide

Use only with the actual rendered paper. If only extracted text or source is available, do not make claims about page appearance, overflow, legibility, density, or image quality.

## Page pass

Inspect every page at whole-page scale for hierarchy, excessive density or emptiness, awkward breaks, float displacement, clipped or overflowing content, isolated headings, large uninterrupted formula blocks, and imbalanced whitespace. Then inspect figures, tables, and small text at readable scale.

## Figures and tables

Check whether each important visual has a readable size, axis labels, units, legend, caption, meaningful precision, distinguishable colors/markers, and an immediately recoverable takeaway. Confirm that the body interprets the visual rather than merely referencing its number. Recommend a chart/table conversion only when it improves comparison or exact-value lookup.

## Consistency

Check fonts, notation, caption treatment, table style, decimal precision, line width, color semantics, and multi-panel spacing across the document. Treat isolated inconsistency as LOW unless it creates ambiguity or misinterpretation.

## Reporting

Name the page and figure/table/equation when possible, describe what is visibly wrong, explain the information cost, and specify the smallest layout or labeling correction. Apply:

```text
clarity > decoration
information density > ornament
consistency > novelty
```
