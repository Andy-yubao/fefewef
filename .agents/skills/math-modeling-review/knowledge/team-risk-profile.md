# Team Risk Profile

This static profile comes from MCM 2026 feedback. It changes attention, not conclusions: report a problem only when the current paper supplies evidence for it.

## Elevated attention

### Executive Summary

Test whether a non-specialist judge can quickly identify the task, approach, most important quantitative findings, and final recommendation. Look especially for unfocused prompt restatement and missing concrete results.

### Model Cohesion & Integration

Trace how every model contributes to the overall answer. Look especially for locally successful analyses whose outputs are never combined or passed downstream.

### Sensitivity Analysis

Within Robustness, identify the inputs, assumptions, or parameters that can alter the final decision. Look especially for absent perturbation tests, unexplained ranges, and sensitivity figures that never state whether the recommendation changes.

### Appropriate Explanations

Test whether consequential modeling choices explain why they fit this task and data. Look especially at parameter values, weights, normalization, thresholds, model selection, and boundary/initial conditions.

## Historical strengths

- Model Selection: prior work explained method–problem fit and input/model adaptation relatively well.
- Model Implementation: prior work implemented models meaningfully and discussed results, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Tables & Figures: prior visuals supported analysis and were explained in the body.

Historical strength does not exempt the current paper from review. Check these areas normally, and avoid needless visual decoration that could damage established clarity.
