# Section 7 image-generation prompts

## Prompt A - realistic search scene

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide paper-opening illustration
Primary request: A realistic outdoor scene showing a four-legged robotic dog searching for a radio-frequency/electromagnetic interference source. The robot is actively performing signal localization rather than patrol or delivery.
Scene/backdrop: A believable open field-edge or industrial-perimeter environment with subtle terrain, a few distant structures, and a small suspected signal-source device in the distance.
Subject: A rugged quadruped robot dog equipped with a compact directional antenna and sensing module; a restrained directional indicator, ground search path, and faint radio-signal cue should make the localization task clear.
Style/medium: high-quality editorial technology photography, realistic materials and scale, suitable for the opening of a mathematical-modeling paper.
Composition/framing: horizontal wide shot, robot in the foreground or left-center, clear depth toward the suspected source, enough uncluttered space for a paper caption.
Lighting/mood: natural daylight, formal and focused, restrained technology atmosphere.
Color palette: natural greens, earth tones, graphite metal, muted blue instrumentation accents.
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no extra people required, no weapon imagery.
Avoid: ordinary pet dog, bipedal robot, delivery scene, patrol pose, exaggerated science fiction, cyberpunk neon, fantasy machinery, oversaturated colors.

## Prompt B - standalone artistic mathematical abstraction (right image)

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: standalone artistic abstract illustration for the right image in a two-image paper layout
Primary request: Create an independent artistic abstraction of a quadruped robot searching for a radio/electromagnetic interference source and transforming the search task into a mathematical localization model. The image must be a complete standalone composition, not the right half of a split-screen image.
Scene/backdrop: A clean deep navy analytical background with a subtle coordinate grid, circular construction lines, and elegant technical geometry.
Subject: A small stylized silhouette or line-art icon of a quadruped robot placed unobtrusively near the edge of the composition, connected conceptually to a central interference-source point. Use directional bearing rays, angle-error wedges, multiple observation stations, intersecting feasible regions, geometric constraints, and one circular local-zoom inset connected by thin dashed construction lines.
Style/medium: artistic semi-abstract scientific illustration, combining refined information-graphic geometry with subtle painterly or dimensional visual effects. Elegant, modern, and suitable for a mathematical-modeling competition paper.
Composition/framing: horizontal 3:2 landscape composition; one unified abstract scene; balanced margins; central feasible-region intersection as the focal point; several observation rays converging toward the source; one local-zoom circle; no split-screen, no photographic panel, and no reality-to-abstraction gradient.
Lighting/mood: clear, calm, intelligent, visually engaging but not dramatic.
Color palette: deep navy, slate blue, cyan, teal, amber, and white; translucent colored wedges with clear contrast; avoid excessive neon saturation.
Constraints: communicate observation uncertainty, bearing-error ranges, multi-point intersection, and geometric localization; no readable text, no equations, no logos, no watermark; keep all geometric elements coherent and uncluttered.
Avoid: realistic landscape, lake, mountain, industrial photograph, realistic robot dog, collage, split-screen composition, left-side photographic scene, visual gradient from reality to abstraction, generic flowchart, random decorative icons, cyberpunk neon, excessive labels, and illegible text.
