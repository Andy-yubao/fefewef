# Section 7 right abstract image prompt

## Prompt

Create a standalone artistic abstract scientific illustration for the right image of a paper opening layout. The image must be a complete independent composition, not the right half of a split-screen scene.

Depict the task of a quadruped robot searching for a radio-frequency or electromagnetic interference source, but express it primarily as a mathematical localization model rather than as a realistic scene. The key visual message should be the abstraction of a real search task into geometric observation and localization.

Use a deep navy analytical background with a subtle coordinate grid, faint circular construction lines, and elegant technical geometry. Place a small stylized quadruped robot silhouette or refined line-art icon unobtrusively near the edge of the composition, only as a semantic anchor. The robot must not dominate the image.

Make the visual focus a central interference-source point and the surrounding localization structure: several directional bearing rays converging toward the source, translucent angle-error wedges, multiple observation stations, intersecting feasible regions, geometric constraints, and one circular local-zoom inset connected by thin dashed construction lines. The feasible-region intersection should act as the main focal area.

Style: semi-abstract scientific illustration, blending refined infographic-like geometry with subtle painterly or dimensional effects. Elegant, modern, academically restrained, visually beautiful, and suitable for a mathematical-modeling competition paper. Slightly futuristic, but not flashy.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape. One unified abstract scene, balanced visual hierarchy, no collage, no scene-to-diagram gradient, no photo panel. The geometry should feel coherent, intentional, and uncluttered. Leave modest negative space so the image can sit comfortably in a paper layout.

Color palette: deep navy, slate blue, cyan, teal, amber, and white. Use translucent wedges and clean geometric overlays with clear contrast, but avoid excessive neon intensity.

Important constraints: no readable text, no equations, no labels, no logos, no watermark. All rays, wedges, stations, and feasible regions must be geometrically coherent. The image should clearly communicate bearing observation, uncertainty, multi-point intersection, and mathematical localization.

Avoid realistic landscape, mountain, lake, industrial photo background, photorealistic robot dog, split-screen composition, left-side photographic scene, gradient from reality to abstraction, generic flowchart, random decorative icons, cyberpunk neon, excessive UI clutter, or decorative but meaningless geometry.

## Avoid / Negative Constraints

- no realistic landscape
- no mountain / lake / industrial scene
- no photorealistic robot dog
- no split-screen or collage
- no real-scene-to-diagram gradient
- no generic flowchart
- no random decorative icons
- no cyberpunk neon
- no readable text, formulas, or labels
- no excessive clutter
- robot should be secondary, geometry should be primary

This is the **abstract right-panel image** for section7.

The **left-panel image is the realistic robot-search scene**, and must remain a separate asset.

This prompt is intentionally designed so that the robot is only a semantic anchor, while mathematical localization geometry is the visual subject.
